# FIXED-SCOPE STATEMENT OF WORK (SOW) & COMMERCIAL BOUNDARIES CHECKLIST

Strict scope guardrails preventing scope creep, unpaid support tails, and payment delays across Wave 2 (Local Contractors) and Wave 3 (`$10M–$50M` Mid-Market).

---

## 1. Wave 2 Fixed-Scope SOW (`$750–$1,500` Turnkey Hub + `$149/mo` or `$299/yr` Listing)
- **Fee & Payment Terms:** `$750–$1,500` flat setup (**100% upfront** via Stripe/ACH, or **50% deposit / 50% prior to DNS cutover**). Zero Net-30 terms.
- **Included Deliverables (Fixed):**
  1. **Live in 48 Hours:** High-converting mobile landing hub, verified click-to-call `tel:` routing, TCPA/SB 140 compliant quote form (`privacy.html` & `terms.html` included), instant owner email/push/webhook lead alert, and calendar link.
  2. **Filed Day 1:** Twilio A2P 10DLC Brand + Campaign registration submitted under client's verified EIN + HTTPS web opt-in footer (**explicit 10–15 business day US carrier review SLA**; automated SMS activates only after carrier approval to prevent `Error 30034`).
  3. **Revision Cap:** **One (1) consolidated revision round** within 7 business days of staging preview.
- **Client Ownership & Third-Party Billing:**
  - Client owns their domain registrar account (e.g., Cloudflare Registrar) and pays Twilio / SMS carrier usage fees directly on client card (`$0` vendor pass-through markup).

## 2. Wave 3 Mid-Market Assessment & Retainer SOW (`$1,000` -> `$10K / $15K / $25K/mo`)
- **Entry Wedge (`$1,000` Paid Assessment — 100% Upfront):**
  - Paid 100% upfront via Stripe/ACH prior to kickoff (**100% credited to Month 1 retainer** upon launch).
  - Deliverables: Pre-call estimator workflow survey, 2 structured frontline staff interviews, and a working **Senior Estimator Co-Pilot** FastMCP prototype tested live on **3 redacted client files** with `source_reference`, `confidence`, and `human_verify_flags`.
- **Explicit Exclusions (Out of Assessment Scope):**
  - Legacy on-prem ERP database migrations (Sage 300 CRE, Foundation, Vista Viewpoint SQL schema rewrites), custom multi-entity financial accounting integrations, or cleaning unindexed historical paper archives (**Data-Readiness / Legacy-ERP Exclusion** — scoped separately only if needed).
- **Production Retainer Tiers (30% Bundled Onboarding & Staff Training Included):**
  - **Tier 1 (`$10,000/mo`):** Speed-to-Lead owner/CRM dispatch hub + estimator intake parser + weekly calibration.
  - **Tier 2 (`$15,000/mo` Recommended):** Full FastMCP Senior Estimator Co-Pilot (`<4s` multimodal takeoff draft + citation matrix + `human_verify_flags`) + live CRM dispatch sync + 30% bundled estimator training & SOP rollout.
  - **Tier 3 (`$25,000/mo`):** Multi-branch dedicated runtime, custom pricing-rule engine, and executive telemetry SLA.
- **Billing Invariant:** **Monthly upfront billing (Stripe/ACH autopay). Zero Net-30 invoices.**
